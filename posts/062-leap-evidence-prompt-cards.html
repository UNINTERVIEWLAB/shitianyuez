Show the judgement, not just the coordination

Goertek & ESCAP · Unscripted evidence prompts

2026.11.23

Capabilities, objective and JD connection

Programme Delivery · Consultation & Facilitation · Knowledge Management & Evidence, with supporting links to partnership development and knowledge translation.

Day 6 · 23 November 2026 · 10–15 minutes. Objective: organise an unscripted explanation around one judgement, specific personal actions and a bounded result. This is a set of prompts, not a model answer to memorise.

LEAP connection: coordinating expert submissions, review materials, meetings and follow-up while leaving scientific decisions with authorised experts.

Goertek: responsibilities in natural English

I coordinate a portfolio of business projects across mass production, new product introduction and proposal stages. I monitor project status, communicate customer requirements, coordinate internal inputs and flag delivery risks.

STAR prompt card: one risk, one judgement

  • S: Choose one project and one approaching milestone. What was uncertain?
  • T: What were you personally responsible for monitoring or communicating?
  • A: What signal did you notice? Why did it warrant action? Identify the information checked, the action you took and the person or role you engaged. Use [confirmed detail] where the sequence is not yet documented.
  • R: State the warning or deliverable actually completed. Add a later business outcome only if verified. Do not imply that a warning automatically prevented a delay.

Verbs: monitored, consolidated, clarified, prioritised, flagged, escalated.

Follow-up to consider: What evidence changed your judgement about when to act?

ESCAP: responsibilities in natural English

I supported regional programmes on the inclusive green transition of MSMEs, coordinating partners, agendas and delivery materials and contributing to evaluation and donor reporting.

STAR prompt card: Malaysia study tour

  • S: A multi-day learning exchange brought policymakers, women entrepreneurs and partner institutions together.
  • T: Select one personally owned contribution: partner liaison, agenda/material coordination, evaluation or a donor brief.
  • A: Identify a real coordination or evidence problem; explain your judgement and two concrete actions. Do not turn the programme’s full task list into your personal responsibility.
  • R: Name the output you completed and the activity result it supported. Keep proposed follow-up cooperation separate from implemented collaboration.

Verbs: liaised, aligned, prepared, consolidated, analysed, synthesised.

Follow-up to consider: Which part of the result can be traced to your contribution, and which belongs to the wider team?

Apply the logic to LEAP without claiming technical expertise

In a hypothetical feed-guideline revision, a missing dataset or conflicting review input may affect a chapter deadline. Explain the coordination logic: identify the dependency, clarify the request, seek the authorised technical decision, record the outcome and follow up the revised deliverable. New ingredients, mitigation technologies and cases require specialist review; transferable coordination experience does not make the coordinator an LCA or feed expert.

For outreach or training, distinguish the completed material from understanding, adoption or environmental outcomes. Evidence discipline improves both reporting and spontaneous explanation.

Five reusable expressions

  • identify the dependency behind a delivery risk
  • clarify my responsibility within the wider team
  • explain the evidence behind a judgement
  • separate the completed output from the intended outcome
  • transfer coordination skills without claiming technical authority

Private comparison exercise · 5–10 minutes

Speak for 60–90 seconds about one Goertek incident without looking at the card. Repeat for one ESCAP contribution. Then check: Did I state the judgement and its basis? Did I name personal actions? Is the result observable? Did I separate team outcomes and subsequent recommendations?

Write only five keywords per card and record a second attempt. Leave unknown details as placeholders. Do not polish this into a fixed script.

Completed cards, recordings, customer details, commercial information and internal processes remain private. This public page contains only general responsibilities and practice prompts.
